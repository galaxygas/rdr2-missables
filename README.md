# RED DEAD REDEMPTION 2 — COMPLETE MISSABLES GUIDE
### Every permanently missable thing in Story Mode, ordered by story progress %

**Compiled:** 14 September 2026
**Scope:** Story Mode (single player) only. Red Dead Online is not covered.
**Platform:** Applies to PS4/PS5, Xbox One/Series, PC. Two entries are patch-dependent and are flagged.

---

## HOW TO READ THIS GUIDE

### The % system used here

RDR2 has **110 story missions across 8 chapters** (Chapter 1–6 + Epilogue Parts 1 and 2), per GTABase's full chronological mission list. Every deadline in this guide is expressed as:

> **Mission #N of 110 → ~X% of the story**

**Important honesty note:** this percentage is *story-mission progress*, NOT the game's in-game "Total Completion %" counter (which tracks the 100% checklist: challenges, collectibles, compendium, etc.). Those are two completely different numbers. Story-mission % is the one that actually governs missables, because everything in this guide expires based on *story position*, not on completion percentage.

### Chapter boundaries by %

| Chapter | Missions | Story % range | Camp |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chapter 1 | 1–6 | 0% → 5% | Colter |
| Chapter 2 | 7–25 | 5% → 23% | Horseshoe Overlook |
| Chapter 3 | 26–43 | 23% → 39% | Clemens Point |
| Chapter 4 | 44–59 | 39% → 54% | Shady Belle |
| Chapter 5 | 60–68 | 54% → 62% | Guarma / Lakay |
| Chapter 6 | 69–89 | 62% → 81% | Beaver Hollow |
| Epilogue 1 | 90–99 | 81% → 90% | Pronghorn Ranch |
| Epilogue 2 | 100–110 | 90% → 100% | Beecher's Hope |

### Symbols

- 🔴 **HARD LOCK** — gone forever past this point, no workaround
- 🟠 **ONE CHANCE** — one interaction only; if you walk away without looting/picking up, it's gone
- 🟡 **CONDITIONAL** — requires a prior choice, honor level, or prior mission
- 🏆 — counts toward a missable Achievement/Trophy
- ⚠️ **DISPUTED** — sources conflict; see the Disputed section at the end

## ⛔ THE 8 HARD DEADLINES — MEMORISE THESE

These are the specific missions that slam doors shut. Do **not** start them until the relevant chapter's checklist below is clear.

| # | Deadline mission | Mission # | Story % | What dies |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Eastward Bound** (end of Ch1) | 6 | 5% | Chapter 1 / Colter is unreachable |
| 2 | **The Sheep and the Goats** → **A Strange Kindness** (end of Ch2) | 24–25 | 22–23% | All Ch2 camp activities, Ch2 item requests, Ch2 honor missions |
| 3 | **Blood Feuds, Ancient and Modern** → **The Battle of Shady Belle** (end of Ch3) | 42–43 | 38–39% | All Ch3 camp activities/requests/honor missions, Clemens Point camp |
| 4 | **Banking, the Old American Art** (end of Ch4) | 59 | **54%** | 🏆 **Give to the Poor, Errand Boy, Friends With Benefits become impossible.** All Ch4 activities/requests/honor missions |
| 5 | **Paradise Mercifully Departed** (end of Guarma) | 64 | 58% | The entire island of Guarma + all 9 Guarma-exclusive animals |
| 6 | **The Fine Art of Conversation** | 82 | 75% | "Of Men and Angels" (per Gamer Guides) |
| 7 | **My Last Boy** | 87 | 79% | "Money Lending and Other Sins VI & VII" (per Steam "Story Mode Missables") |
| 8 | **Our Best Selves** — 🔴 **THE POINT OF NO RETURN** | 88 | **80%** | **All free roam as Arthur ends.** Every remaining Arthur-only item, mission, honor mission, camp thing, cash and horses |

**One more soft deadline:** finishing **Red Dead Redemption** (#89, 81%) ends Arthur permanently. After that you are John for the rest of the game.

> **The single most important rule in this document:** make a **separate manual save slot** right before **Our Best Selves** (80%). Rockstar's own guides and GTABase both recommend this. It is the only way back if you miss something Arthur-only.

## 🏆 THE 4 MISSABLE ACHIEVEMENTS / TROPHIES

PowerPyx: *"Rockstar threw in 4 missable trophies which are related to your camp from chapter 2 – 4 and for doing all optional (missable) story missions."*

| Trophy | Requirement | Available from | 🔴 DEAD AFTER |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Give to the Poor** | Donate **$250 total** to the gang tithing box | Eastward Bound (5%) | **Banking, the Old American Art (54%)** |
| **Errand Boy** | Deliver **5** camp companion item requests | Eastward Bound (5%) | **Banking, the Old American Art (54%)** |
| **Friends With Benefits** | Complete **a Companion Activity in each camp** (Horseshoe Overlook, Clemens Point, Shady Belle) | Eastward Bound (5%) | **Banking, the Old American Art (54%)** |
| **Lending a Hand** | Complete **all optional Honor story missions** | Chapter 2 | **Our Best Selves (80%)** — spread across Ch2, 3, 4 and 6 |

**Three of the four die at 54% of the story.** If you blow past Chapter 4 without touching the camp, the Platinum / 1000G requires a full second playthrough.

**Note on Lending a Hand:** PSNProfiles counts **28 individual optional honor missions**. A post-1.22 exploit exists — replaying the Epilogue 2 mission **"Home Improvement for Beginners"** (or **"Old Habits"**) has been reported to pop the trophy retroactively. Treat that as a backup, not a plan; it is an unpatched bug, not intended behaviour.

**Also note:** the in-game **100% Completion checklist ("Best in the West")** does NOT require anything in this guide. The Red Dead Wiki states plainly that *"anything on this list is not required for 100% Completion."* Missables matter for the **trophy list** and for **true completionism**, not for the in-game percentage counter.

# CHAPTER 5 — GUARMA
### Missions 60–68 · 54% → 62% of the story
### 🔴🔴 **THE WHOLE ISLAND IS MISSABLE.** Last chance before **Paradise Mercifully Departed** (#64, 58%)

> RDR2.org states it bluntly: *"After this chapter finishes you will not be able to return to this chapter... This makes pretty much every mission in this chapter missable entirely."*
> There is **no legitimate way back to Guarma.** (Glitches exist; they are not officially supported and can corrupt saves.)

## 🔴 Guarma-exclusive animals — 9 total

**Study** each with binoculars (R1/RB) **and** kill + skin/pluck for a full Compendium entry.

**Macaws (3)** — spawn near the **waterfall by the camp**, and land on rocks around Arroyo De La Vibora:
1. **Blue and Yellow Macaw**
2. **Scarlet Macaw**
3. **Great Green Macaw**

**Boas (3)** — spawn in the **vegetation down the hill from camp** / small clearing NW of camp:
4. **Sunglow Boa Snake**
5. **Rainbow Boa Snake**
6. **Red-Tailed Boa Snake** (listed as "Red Boa" in some trophy guides)

**Birds (1)**
7. **Red-Footed Booby** — mostly stays airborne; often flies above the river

**Snakes (1)**
8. **Fer-de-Lance Snake** — ⚠️ **Disputed.** PSNProfiles lists it as Guarma-only; the Red Dead Wiki says it **can also be found at Stillwater Creek** on the mainland. Get it on Guarma to be safe.

**Bonus, no Compendium entry (9)**
9. **Green Turtle** — has **no Compendium slot** and cannot normally be seen during gameplay. Studying one only adds a **journal drawing**. Practically unobtainable without glitches.

> ✅ **Good news, confirmed:** PowerPyx, PSNProfiles and the PlayStationTrophies guide all confirm **none of the Guarma animals are required** for 🏆 **Zoologist**, 🏆 **Skin Deep**, or the 100% checklist. They count as *extras* that let you skip an equivalent number of mainland animals. Missing them blocks **no trophy**.
>
> **Also confirmed:** **Cuban Land Crab** and **Green Iguana** are **NOT missable** — both can be found on the mainland (the largest island in Flat Iron Lake).

### 💰 The money workaround (Aberdeen Pig Farm)

A widely-used community method to carry Arthur's cash into the Epilogue:
1. Go to **Aberdeen Pig Farm as Arthur** — this only works if you have **never** triggered the encounter before.
2. Accept the invitation, agree to everything, get drugged, wake up in a ditch robbed.
3. **Do not go back for the money.** Leave the area.
4. Finish the story, complete the Epilogue 1 mission **"Motherhood"** (#97, 88%), then return and collect from behind the portrait.

> **Do this after "My Last Boy" (79%)** so you're stashing your final total. ⚠️ Some players report Rockstar patched this; others confirmed it still worked as of recent years. **Treat it as unreliable — the guaranteed method is simply to spend the money on guns and clothing, both of which transfer.**

# 📰 NEWSPAPERS & NEWSPAPER-UNLOCKED CHEATS — THE FULL ANSWER

You asked specifically about newspaper unlocks, so here is the verified position:

## ✅ Newspapers themselves are NOT missable

Multiple sources confirm back issues stay on sale:

- *"Good news for completionists: no newspaper is missable. Even if you progress to Epilogue 2 without having bought any previous issues, vendors will still offer all the old issues alongside the new ones."* — Mundobytes
- *"No newspaper edition has been confirmed as permanently missable based on story progression."* — GamesRead
- Steam community confirmation: *"Don't worry you won't miss any issue. All issues you do not have will be available to buy at every vendor."*

**There are 6 newspaper vendor locations.** New editions unlock as story events happen. The three papers are the **New Hanover Gazette** (Valentine), the **Blackwater Ledger** (Blackwater/Strawberry) and the **Saint Denis Times** (Saint Denis/Rhodes).

**Why people think they're missable:** vendors have **opening hours** (come back midday), and **you cannot buy a paper you already own** until a new issue releases. Both look like a locked-out vendor but aren't.

## ⚠️ Newspaper cheats: owning the paper is only half the requirement

Per Pixel Twelve: *"Owning a late newspaper does not unlock every gated phrase automatically. Your save must also be past the required mission, chapter, or Epilogue point."*

If you enter a gated cheat too early, the game returns **"You don't meet the prerequisites to unlock this cheat."** That is a story-progress gate, not a missed newspaper.

**Critical warning:** activating **any** cheat **disables manual saving and blocks all Trophy/Achievement progress for that session.** Do not touch cheats until after you've earned the missable trophies.

## 🟠 The ONE genuinely missable newspaper-related item

The Red Dead Wiki lists a document lootable from **newspaper boys**, with two traps:

1. *"If the player buys all of the available newspapers, the newspaper boys will no longer spawn and this document will no longer be obtainable."*
2. *"The player cannot loot the letter from the kid anymore after some patches that stop the player from killing kids. If the player wants to obtain it, they have to downgrade... to patch 1.0 (works only on the console versions)."*

> **Honest caveat:** the source I verified describes this item but I could not confirm its exact in-game name from a second independent source, so I'm not going to invent one. Practically: on any modern patch this document is **unobtainable regardless**, so it does not affect a normal playthrough.

# 💾 SAVE STRATEGY — the practical insurance policy

RDR2 autosaves after choices, purchases, deaths and travel, so **one autosave is not enough protection**.

**Make a dedicated manual save at each of these points:**

1. **Start of Chapter 2** (5%) — before the densest missable block
2. **Before "The Sheep and the Goats"** (22%) — Chapter 2 final check
3. **Before "Blood Feuds, Ancient and Modern"** (38%) — Chapter 3 final check

5. **On arrival in Guarma** (54%) — before the animals are gone forever
6. **Before "Our Best Selves"** (**80%**) — 🔴 **the point of no return; the single most important save in the game**
7. **Before entering Francis Sinclair's cabin** (Old Brass Compass)
8. **Before handing Pearson the rabbit** (glitchy Scout Jacket)
9. **Before entering the Wild Man's cave** (glitchy journal)

> **A chapter save turns a missed camp request into a short reload instead of a 50-hour replay.**

**One more rule:** replaying a time-sensitive mission later from the Progress menu **does not always credit the reward or trophy progress to your main save.** Mission replay is for **Gold Medals**, not for fixing missables. (The one exception is the Lending a Hand replay bug noted earlier.)

### A closing note on completeness

The Red Dead Wiki itself opens its missables page with this disclaimer, and it applies here too:

> *"As there is no official list of everything that's missable, this list may be missing some content or certain content may be available even outside these time-frames."*

Rockstar has never published a missables list. This guide consolidates the most exhaustive community sources that exist, cross-checked against each other, with conflicts flagged rather than hidden. If a single item somewhere is undocumented by every source above, no guide on the internet has it either.

**The three things that actually matter:** save before **Banking, the Old American Art** (54%), save before **Our Best Selves** (80%), and never leave a camp without clearing its checklist.
